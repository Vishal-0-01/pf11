Asset-class allocation
Rule: equity = clip(0.70 − 0.10 × z, 0.50, 0.90), where z is the average of the P/E and P/B z-scores. The fund weights (3–25% each) and the debt/gold split of the remainder maximise expected return, with portfolio volatility held at or below 10% (hard cap; no minimum for debt or gold). If the cap cannot be met at the rule's equity weight, equity is lowered (never below 50%) and flagged.
Portfolio composition
Allocation decided each 31 December from the Nifty 50 P/E and P/B reading on that date, using only data available at that date. Equity target is the valuation rule; equity final is after the 10% volatility check on trailing 3-year data. Allocation only, no returns.
Frontier across equity weights of 50–90% for the fund sleeve and defensive assets, without the 10% cap applied; the star marks the current recommended portfolio and the dashed line the 10% cap.
